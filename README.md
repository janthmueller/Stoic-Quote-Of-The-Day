    <em><!--START_SECTION:quote-text-->
You must vie with time's swiftness in the speed of using it, and, as from a torrent that rushes by and will not always flow, you must drink quickly.
<!--END_SECTION:quote-text--></em><br>
    <strong>— <!--START_SECTION:quote-author-->
Seneca
<!--END_SECTION:quote-author--></strong>

<div align="center" style="max-width:600px;margin:0 auto;">
<!--START_SECTION:quote-interpretation-->
In our fast-paced world, time often feels like it's slipping through our fingers, much like a river that rushes past us and never returns. Seneca's wisdom reminds us to be intentional about how we use every fleeting moment. Instead of letting days blur by in a haze of routine and distraction, we should actively engage with our time, making the most of each opportunity it presents. Imagine life as a swift-moving stream where grasping at passing opportunities requires both awareness and agility. In this light, scrolling endlessly through social media or postponing that call to a loved one becomes a missed sip from an uncatchable current. We are encouraged to live with a sense of urgency balanced with mindfulness, seizing moments to enrich our lives with meaningful actions and experiences. By appreciating the swift passage of time, like savoring each drop of a refreshing drink, we foster a sharper presence and a more profound fulfillment. In our modern lives, this principle nudges us to prioritize what truly matters, making each day count in the limited time we've been granted.
