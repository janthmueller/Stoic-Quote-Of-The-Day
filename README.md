<p align="center">
    <em><!--START_SECTION:quote-text-->
The best kind of revenge is not to become like them.
<!--END_SECTION:quote-text--></em><br>
    <strong>— <!--START_SECTION:quote-author-->
Marcus Aurelius
<!--END_SECTION:quote-author--></strong>
</p>

<div align="center" style="max-width:600px;margin:0 auto;">
<!--START_SECTION:quote-interpretation-->
In a world where anger and hostility can be easily met with the same, the wisdom of Marcus Aurelius provides a guiding light for those seeking peace and strength in difficult interactions. When someone acts hurtfully or unjustly towards us, it's natural to feel the urge to retaliate or mirror their behavior. However, taking revenge only perpetuates a cycle of negativity and diminishes our own character. By choosing not to become consumed by these negative impulses or to embody the same harmful traits, we rise above the situation. Instead of allowing someone else's misbehavior to dictate our actions, we can focus on maintaining our own integrity and values. This approach not only preserves our peace but also often disarms the aggressor, as they find no fuel for their fire. In contemporary circumstances, whether it's a workplace conflict, a betrayal by a friend, or an unkind stranger on the internet, opting to respond with kindness, patience, or simply by not engaging, demonstrates true strength and character. In this way, not only do we protect our own serenity and self-respect, but we also offer others an example of how to navigate life's challenges gracefully.
<!--END_SECTION:quote-interpretation-->
