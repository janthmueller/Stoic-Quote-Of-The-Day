<p align="center">
    <em><!--START_SECTION:quote-text-->
No man will ever be happy if tortured by the greater happiness of another.
<!--END_SECTION:quote-text--></em><br>
    <strong>— <!--START_SECTION:quote-author-->
Seneca
<!--END_SECTION:quote-author--></strong>
</p>

<div align="center" style="max-width:600px;margin:0 auto;">
<!--START_SECTION:quote-interpretation-->
Seneca's wisdom speaks to the self-defeating nature of jealousy and the focus on external comparisons that can detract from our personal contentment. In today's fast-paced world, it's easy to fall into the trap of evaluating our self-worth based on others' achievements, lifestyles, or social media portrayals. If we incessantly measure our happiness against someone else's success, we surrender our peace and satisfaction to something beyond our control. True happiness stems from managing our own desires and cultivating gratitude for what we already have. Rather than being swayed by comparisons, nurturing a mindset that finds joy in personal growth and authentic connections fosters inner peace. By shifting our focus inward and appreciating our unique journey, we free ourselves from the torment of envy, allowing us to find contentment that is stable and lasting.
<!--END_SECTION:quote-interpretation-->
