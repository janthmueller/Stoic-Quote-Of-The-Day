    <em><!--START_SECTION:quote-text-->
Never let the future disturb you. You will meet it, if you have to, with the same weapons of reason which today arm you against the present.
<!--END_SECTION:quote-text--></em><br>
    <strong>— <!--START_SECTION:quote-author-->
Marcus Aurelius
<!--END_SECTION:quote-author--></strong>
</p>

<div align="center" style="max-width:600px;margin:0 auto;">
<!--START_SECTION:quote-interpretation-->
In a world that's always planning ahead, it's easy to fall into the trap of worrying about the future. Marcus Aurelius reminds us to approach life's uncertainties with the tools of reason we use each day. It's a gentle yet powerful reminder that the skills and mental strategies we apply to today's problems—like clear thinking, patience, and rational analysis—are equally effective when facing tomorrow’s challenges. Contemporary life often bombards us with predictions of economic downturns or personal what-ifs that can create anxiety. Yet, just as we calmly navigate current issues by focusing on what we can control and addressing challenges one step at a time, we can meet future obstacles in the same way. By emphasizing the strength of our existing capabilities, Aurelius encourages us not to fear what lies ahead, but to trust in our ability to handle it thoughtfully and wisely. This approach fosters resilience and peace of mind in the midst of a rapidly changing and unpredictable world.
<!--END_SECTION:quote-interpretation-->
